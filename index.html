How Participation Works

Six steps from joining Ping to building your project

One shared identity connects your participation on Ping, your learning on DOT, and your project execution in GRIOT AI.

1Ping

Join Ping and verify your account.

Create your primary builder profile on Ping, complete account verification, and receive your permanent DOT ID.

2Ping

Share your idea and participate.

Register for the Challenge using your Ping profile, submit your idea, and engage with the builder community.

3Ping → DOT

Earn eligible DOT credits progressively.

Unlock learning credits step by step as you verify your account, complete learning milestones, and demonstrate project progress.

4DOT

Learn and develop on DOT.

Open DOT with your linked identity, activate your credit wallet, and take structured courses, learning paths, and assessments.

5GRIOT AI

Qualify for GRIOT AI through eligibility and merit-based selection.

Meet programme eligibility requirements and pass merit-based review to earn access to the GRIOT AI execution workspace.

6GRIOT AI → Ping

Build your project and share progress on Ping.

Structure your project, complete tasks and milestones in GRIOT AI, and publish your approved progress back on Ping.

The Challenge

Participation, verification, progressive credits, and selection

Build Africa Challenge is a builder development programme rewarding real learning and demonstrated execution.

Account Verification Tiers

Platform verification confirms your builder identity on Ping and triggers permanent DOT ID issuance. Standard: Free, Builder Plus: ₦3,000, Builder Pro: ₦30,000.

GRIOT AI Eligibility & Merit-Based Selection

Two gates: Programme Eligibility and Merit-Based Selection. Access cannot be purchased and is not promised to every participant. Spotlight on Ping is also strictly merit-based without an application button.
