The problem worth solving

Africa has builders. They need a better way to find each other.

Talent is not the missing ingredient. The missing piece is connected infrastructure that helps people move from isolated ambition to shared progress.

↗Potential collaborators are spread across disconnected social platforms and private groups.
↗Opportunities, knowledge and practical support are difficult to discover in one place.
↗Good ideas often struggle to find the people and execution support needed to become real solutions.
Our thesis

Connection should lead somewhere.

Ping is designed around productive intent: help people find the right community, start useful conversations, share what they are building and access a wider system for learning and execution. The goal is not simply more screen time. It is more meaningful work made possible through a connected network.

Founder & builder

From building communities to building the network.

Dum Victor is an entrepreneur and venture builder focused on creating systems that help people turn ideas into real-world progress. For approximately four years, he has been building Avantage Group around the conviction that talent grows faster when people have access to the right networks, knowledge and execution infrastructure.

Ping was built from that conviction. Developed largely hands-on as a solo-founder effort, it is now moving from beta toward a wider public launch and a broader team across product, engineering, security, infrastructure, growth and community operations.

His experience spans student representation, community leadership and entrepreneurship. He has served as a Student Representative at Wigwe University and as a regional lead within the Redeemed Christian Church of God, alongside leadership and management roles in other communities.

He has also been nominated by the ISMEA Council for a doctorate degree in Entrepreneurship and Venture Creation at the University of Thames, Paris, France. This is a nomination, not a claim that the degree has been awarded.
