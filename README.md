# BUILD AFRICA CHALLENGE

**Have an idea? Let's help you build it.**

Join Ping. Share your idea. Learn on DOT. If selected, build with GRIOT AI.

- **Programme:** Build Africa Challenge
- **Target Launch:** December 2026
- **One-Year Objective:** Bring **100 million people** into one connected builder network.
- **Products:** Ping (`justping.lovable.app`), DOT (`dotlive.lovable.app`), and GRIOT AI
- **Operating Sequence:** `Ping → DOT → GRIOT AI → Ping`

---

## 1. The Three Products

### PING — JOIN AND CONNECT
Discover opportunities, join communities, share ideas, connect with builders and publish progress.
- **Live Product:** https://justping.lovable.app/
- **Role in the Network:** Ping connects people and distributes opportunities.
- **Why Builders Return:** Discovery, communities, connections, opportunities and progress.

### DOT — LEARN AND DEVELOP
Access courses, learning paths, resources, learning progress, a credit wallet and qualifying perks.
- **Live Product:** https://dotlive.lovable.app/
- **Role in the Network:** DOT develops people's knowledge and capabilities.
- **Why Builders Return:** Courses, learning paths, assessments, resources and learning progress.

### GRIOT AI — PLAN AND BUILD
A project execution workspace that helps selected builders structure projects, organise tasks, schedule work, track milestones and improve execution.
- **Role in the Network:** GRIOT AI helps people turn knowledge and ideas into execution.
- **Why Builders Return:** Project workspaces, tasks, schedules, milestones and execution history.

---

## 2. How Participation Works

1. **Join Ping and verify your account.**  
   Create your primary builder profile on Ping, complete account verification, and receive your permanent DOT ID.
2. **Share your idea and participate.**  
   Register for the Challenge using your Ping profile, submit your idea, and engage with the builder community.
3. **Earn eligible DOT credits progressively.**  
   Unlock learning credits step by step as you verify your account, complete learning milestones, and demonstrate project progress.
4. **Learn and develop on DOT.**  
   Open DOT with your linked identity, activate your credit wallet, and take structured courses, learning paths, and assessments.
5. **Qualify for GRIOT AI through eligibility and merit-based selection.**  
   Meet programme eligibility requirements and pass merit-based review to earn access to the GRIOT AI execution workspace.
6. **Build your project and share progress on Ping.**  
   Structure your project, complete tasks and milestones in GRIOT AI, and publish your approved progress back on Ping.

---

## 3. Network Strategy

These are **three connected products, not isolated applications**:
- **Ping** connects people and distributes opportunities.
- **DOT** develops people's knowledge and capabilities.
- **GRIOT AI** helps people turn knowledge and ideas into execution.

Identity, learning progress, project activity and approved progress sharing connect the experience. Each product remains independently useful: a user can use Ping without DOT, learn on DOT without participating in the Challenge, and use GRIOT AI independently when granted access.

## 4. The Challenge Structure

### Account Verification Tiers
- **Standard:** Free — Verified Ping identity and permanent DOT ID issuance.
- **Builder Plus:** ₦3,000 — Verified identity with expanded programme eligibility.
- **Builder Pro:** ₦30,000 — Full professional builder verification tier.

### Progressive, Tiered DOT Credit Rewards
Participants earn eligible DOT credits progressively as they meet real milestones. DOT credits support learning and development on DOT—they are not cash, investment capital, or withdrawable funds.
- **Tier 1 — Verified Builder:** Verified Ping account, DOT ID issued, and qualifying active participation.
- **Tier 2 — Learning Builder:** Required DOT learning milestones completed.
- **Tier 3 — Active Builder:** Meaningful project progress and evidence submitted.
- **Tier 4 — Demonstrating Builder:** Approved project milestone or deliverable.
- **Tier 5 — Exceptional Builder:** Merit-based programme review and additional approved achievement.

### GRIOT AI Eligibility & Merit-Based Selection
Access to GRIOT AI is not guaranteed to every participant and cannot be purchased. Builders qualify through two gates:
1. **Gate 1 — Programme Eligibility:** Verified Ping account, linked DOT ID, active idea submission, and required DOT learning progress.
2. **Gate 2 — Merit-Based Selection:** Evaluation of idea quality, consistency, submitted evidence, and execution readiness.

### Editorial Spotlight
Spotlight recognises standout builders on Ping through merit-based editorial selection. Users do not apply for Spotlight. Selection considers idea quality, originality, evidence of real progress, consistency, meaningful impact, execution quality, and authentic engagement.

## 5. Roadmap to December 2026 Launch

1. **Connect Ping and DOT identities:** Enable single sign-in so verified Ping members automatically receive their permanent DOT ID and can claim their account safely.
2. **Make DOT Learn and its credit wallet ready:** Put learning paths and courses front and centre on DOT, connected to the credit wallet and qualifying perks.
3. **Build the GRIOT AI project workspace:** Deliver the Project Architect, task planner, work review tools, and DOT learning recommendations for selected builders.
4. **Connect the complete Challenge journey:** Link idea submissions, progressive credit rewards, learning milestones, and approved progress updates across all three products.
5. **Test and launch:** Verify the end-to-end builder experience and launch the Build Africa Challenge in December 2026.
